Dziewczyny
Maszyny Event Design
Scope of work
Visual identity,
Digital design for social media,
Typeface,
Motion design
The visual identity of the mentoring program organized by the Dziewczyny-Maszyny group, which supports writing and publishing texts. The theme of this edition is the word SLOWLY. The meaning of the slogan was captured using a script typeface, reflecting the time and dedication needed to create a character. The script typeface SLOWLY creates various patterns, reflecting the complexity and patience involved in the creative process.
Supplement packaging & promotion
Scope of work
Packaging,
Digital design for social media
Have a G Day is more than just a brand of supplements; it is a proposal for a daily ritual and a way of taking care of yourself. The brand has been designed to be not only effective but also visually appealing. It talks about balance and well-being in an accessible and fresh way. The packaging was created on this basis, referring to the requirements set out in the brand book.

Sad Typography
Scope of work
Visual identity,
Digital design for social media,
Typeface,
Video recording,
Video editing
Sad Dances are contemporary dance classes that allow participants to release their emotions, acting as a kind of catharsis. The visuals of the moving poster are a tribute to the organic movements of dance and refer to the nature-inspired, wavy, and dynamic shapes known from Art Nouveau.
Circular Economy Explainer Video
Scope of work
Stop-motion animation,
Digital design,
Motion design,
Video editing
Explanatory video, which attempts to turn our inaction in the face of environmental disaster into action. The project aimed to present the idea of the natural system as a whole, of which we are an integral part, using the concept of the circular economy. Using stop-motion animation helps to create an atmosphere that feels closer and more grounded in reality. Motion design is the main element used to explain the discussed content.
Wine label Winnica Łukasz
Scope of work
Brand identity,
Packaging
The wine label was created for a family winery with deep agricultural roots. The project was about combining nurturing relationships between people and nature and honoring the cultivation experience that describes the vineyard and its owners. This is depicted in the graphic as a combination of nine unique fields (nine family members) forming color transitions (relationships). The colors are chosen to match the character of the wine.
